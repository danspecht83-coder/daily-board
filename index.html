🌟 Welcome to the Daily Board!
A daily management app for children that builds routines, tracks mood, earns rewards, and keeps parents connected. Everything saves automatically and resets fresh each morning.
🚀 Getting Started
When you first open the app you will be prompted to enter your child's name. This creates their personal board. You can add up to 6 children later from the Parent screen under Manage Children.

The board title automatically shows the child's name - for example Sophia's Daily Board. When multiple children are set up, each child sees their own name when they tap their avatar on the user selection screen.

Everything resets automatically each morning - daily tasks and chores clear overnight so the board is fresh every day without any manual work.
📅 Daily Tab
The main screen your child sees every day. Shows their daily tasks, a mood check-in, and their Star Bank balance at the top.

How tasks work: Tap a task to mark it as done - it goes to Waiting... status until a parent approves it. If tapped by accident, tap it again while it says "Waiting... (tap to undo)" to cancel the request. Once a parent approves it, it locks as done for the day.

Mood check-in: Five faces from Awful to Great. Tap a face to log how you feel - a note box appears where you can optionally write why. You can log your mood as many times as you like throughout the day.
🏭 Chores Tab
Weekly chores organised by day of the week. Works exactly like daily tasks - tap to submit for approval, tap again while pending to undo an accidental tap, parent approves or rejects. Only today's chores are shown by default.
🛡 Rules Tab
A read-only list of behaviour expectations for your child to reference any time. Set and edit these from the Parent screen under Manage Behaviour Rules.
🎁 Bonus Tab
Extra behaviours your child can earn bonus stars for - things above and beyond their normal routine. When they do one, they tap Claim! and it goes to the parent for approval. Approved bonuses add stars directly to their Star Bank.
⭐ Rewards Tab
Shows the child's Star Bank balance and a menu of rewards they can spend stars on - things like extra screen time, choosing the movie, or a special treat.

How claiming works: When a child taps Claim! the stars are deducted from their balance immediately so they can see the cost, but the reward shows as Pending approval. The parent then approves or rejects it. If rejected, the stars are refunded automatically. You will never accidentally double-charge.

Manage the reward menu from the Parent screen - add, edit, hide, or remove rewards any time.
🟢 Scale Tab
A three-tier Green / Yellow / Red consequence system shown as a traffic light. Green is a gentle reminder, Yellow removes a privilege, Red is a serious consequence. You write what each tier means for your family from the Parent screen under Manage Scale.
🗺 Outings Tab
Read-only rules about what behaviour is expected to earn or keep an outing. Good to have visible so expectations are clear before you leave the house. Set these from the Parent screen under Manage Outing Rules.
🧍 Calm Tab
A calm-down corner for big feelings. Contains three tools:

• Breathing circle - a guided breathing animation with adjustable timing for inhale, hold, exhale, and number of cycles (tap the gear icon to customise).
• Mood check-in - the same five-face scale as the Daily tab, plus nine more specific feeling chips: Angry, Frustrated, Worried, Nervous, Confused, Embarrassed, Tired, Proud, Excited. All completely separate from the five general moods so there's no overlap. Tapping any of them opens an optional note box.
• Cool down timer - a countdown timer to give structure to a break.

The parent can set feelings tracking to Track (saved and visible to parent), Private (shown on screen but not saved), or Hide (removes the section entirely from the Calm tab).
✅ Choices Tab
Choice boards you create for your child. Each board gives them 2 to 4 options that are all acceptable to you - so they get real control while you keep outcomes in bounds. Particularly helpful for reducing power struggles during transitions. Set up and edit choice boards from the Parent screen.
📅 Progress Tab
A monthly calendar showing coloured dots for activity each day:

• Pink - stars earned   • Light pink - tasks completed   • Purple - reward claimed
• Gold - bonus earned   • Teal - mood logged   • Orange - stars removed

Tap any day to see a full breakdown including the mood timeline with timestamps, any notes written, rewards and bonuses for that day, and if stars were removed - the reason why.
Parent Screen
Accessed by tapping Parent in the header and entering your PIN. The default PIN is 1234 - change it straight away.

When multiple children are set up, coloured child tabs appear at the top of the parent screen so you can instantly switch between children's data without leaving. A red dot on a tab means that child has something waiting for approval.

Pending Approvals - tasks, chores, bonus claims, and reward claims all appear here. Approve or reject each one individually. Approving a task marks it as done on the child's screen. Rejecting a reward claim automatically refunds their stars.

Stars & Streak - one unified card showing the Star Bank balance, total stars ever earned, and the current good behaviour streak. Three ways to award stars:
• Good Day +1★ - awards one star and adds one to the streak count in a single tap
• Add Stars - enter any number of stars plus an optional reason
• Remove Stars - deducts stars with a reason that gets saved to the calendar so you can look back at why

Mood & Behaviour Log - see today's full mood timeline with timestamps and any notes the child wrote. Add your own notes with tags (Trigger, Worked!, Struggle, Win). Run a Collaborative Problem Solving session with three guided fields. The 3-position toggle sets whether feelings are tracked, kept private, or hidden from the child.

Manage Daily Tasks - add, view, and delete daily tasks. Reset all tasks if needed.
Manage Weekly Chores - pick a day of the week and add, view, or delete chores for that day.
Manage Behaviour Rules - add, edit, or delete the rules shown on the child's Rules tab.
Manage Outing Rules - same for the Outings tab.
Manage Reward Menu - add, edit, hide, or remove rewards from the child's Rewards tab.
Manage Choice Boards - create and edit the choices shown on the Choices tab.
Timers & Alarms - set countdown timers and daily repeating alarms. When multiple children are set up, these move to the shared user selection screen so the whole family sees them.
Manage Children - add, rename, or remove children (up to 6). Each child has completely separate data, their own colour scheme, and their own board title.
Colour Scheme - per-child colour theme (12 accent colours and 39 background options).
PIN Settings - change the parent PIN. When multiple children are set up, a Board Title Name field also appears here so you can customise each child's title.
👪 Multiple Children
Add a second child from Parent → Manage Children → + Add a Child. Once a second child exists:

• The app opens to a user selection screen - each child taps their name card to open their board
• A Switch User button appears in the child's header to return to the selection screen
• Timers and alarms move to the shared selection screen so the whole family sees them
• Each child has their own separate tasks, chores, stars, mood log, colour scheme, and progress calendar
• The parent PIN is shared across all children
• The parent screen shows a tab row at the top to quickly switch between children's data
💾 What Gets Saved
Saves permanently: All tasks, chores, stars, mood logs, notes, rewards, streaks, calendar history, colour preferences, alarms, and child profiles. Everything survives closing and reopening the app.

Resets each morning: Task and chore completion status clears automatically when the app is opened on a new day - no manual reset needed.

Resets on close: Active countdown timers and mid-session breathing exercises do not resume after the app is closed. They restart from their set duration.
🔑 Security tip: The default parent PIN is 1234. Change it before giving the device to your child. Go to Parent → PIN Settings → Change PIN.
